## Things this found

- **The hot water runs are long.** The B10 is in the garage, the taps are 1.5–3 m away: ~8 m of hot pipe. Insulate it; ~0.1 L runs cold before hot arrives per metre of 12 mm pipe.
- **The shower is the far end of everything:** the drain pump, both water lines and a light all go through the WC base. Plan that base with a service panel.
- **The starter battery cable is the longest heavy run** (~5.5 m, one way). Fuse it at the starter battery.
- **Nothing wet near the batteries:** all water crosses to the passenger side in the floor at x 1930, the galley's aft end.
