# v2-real — low-budget alternative

Made 2026-09-26. **Not a new layout:** the same van, the same furniture and the same layout as
v2-real, with cheaper products and a few things left out. No viewer, no drawings.

| | v2-real | **Low budget** |
|---|---|---|
| Build total (incl. approval + 10 % reserve) | €19,000–22,900 | **≈ €9,800–12,600** |
| Rough-road pot | €7,360–9,720 | **€0–910** |
| Weight, loaded for a trip | 3,425 kg (74 under) | **3,264 kg (235 under)** |
| Worst week (Morocco, winter, no campsite) | 0 % — 0.6 kWh short | **27 % — ok** |

Details: [budget.md](budget.md) · [weight.md](weight.md) · [energy.md](energy.md) (the last two
are generated by `python lowbudget.py` from the same models as v2-real).

**Surprise:** the low-budget van is **lighter** and holds a **better** energy week. It drops
heavy things (lift, guards, air springs, water heater, oven, 2nd fan) and power-hungry things
(the C95L fridge, the oven, a second fan) — and one 200 Ah battery is enough once those go.

## What we would lose

| Area | What goes | What it means in daily life |
|---|---|---|
| **Hot water** | Truma B10 gas boiler | **No hot tap.** Washing up with water from the kettle; showers from a solar shower bag (warm on sunny days only) |
| **Shower** | the mixer and the Gulper pump | Shower inside from the bag, draining by gravity — the tray must sit above the grey tank inlet |
| **Oven** | Tefal oven | No baking (the Phoenix could not run it anyway: 1,200 W) |
| **230 V power** | MultiPlus C 2,000 VA → Phoenix 1,200 W | No kettle, hair dryer, induction or oven on 230 V. Laptop, monitor, chargers: fine. **No automatic switch** between shore power and the inverter |
| **Battery** | 300 Ah Ective with heating → 200 Ah Power Queen, no heating, no app | Less buffer for a cloudy week (energy check still ok); **must not charge below 0 °C** — fine inside a warm van, a problem in a frozen one; Chinese brand, less support |
| **Monitoring** | Victron app for everything | A mix of apps and displays (Renogy, Junctek) |
| **Sun + engine charging** | separate 30 A MPPT + 50 A DC-DC | One box, **50 A shared**; panels only in parallel |
| **Air** | 2 MaxxFans | **One fan.** Weaker through-draught on hot nights — the thing that replaces air conditioning in Morocco and Turkey |
| **Windows** | Dometic S4 | Carbest acrylic: simpler hinges and latches, feel cheaper. Still E-marked, so legal |
| **Fridge** | Vitrifrigo C95L (95 L, real freezer) | Indel B Cruise 85: **10 L less**, small freezer — but it uses less power |
| **Kitchen sink** | Blanco + insert bowl (soapy-water compartment), Franke pull-out mixer, Alb Nano filter + drinking tap | A plain sink and mixer; **no second compartment**; a carbon filter only — **boil or treat water** from unknown taps |
| **WC** | Porta Potti 565E | Porta Potti 335: lower seat (41 cm), smaller waste tank (12 L), no electric flush |
| **Noise** | sound deadening | The body panels **drum more** on rough roads |
| **Walls** | 6 mm poplar on walls and ceiling | Felt on the walls: **nothing to screw hooks into**, less tough, harder to clean |
| **Rough roads** | Seikel lift, engine and diff guards, air springs | **30 mm less clearance**, no underbody protection on rocky tracks, rear sits lower when loaded. Can be added later — nothing in the build depends on it |
| **Tyres** | BFGoodrich KO2 | General Grabber AT3: not the 3PMSF snow mark in this size — check the winter rules in DE and AT |
| **Fly screen** | VanQuito for the sliding door | A DIY magnetic net |

## What stays the same

- The layout, furniture, dinette bed, shower cabin and wardrobe.
- **Gas cooking** (a cheaper hob, still with flame-failure devices) — a certified installer and revision.
- The **fresh tank** stays made to size: no stock tank fits the 374 mm width.
- **Starlink Mini** — it is the work internet. Dropping it saves ~€250 + the monthly plan, but then
  there is no internet away from mobile signal.
- The approval: the same steps and fees.

## If we mix

The two versions share everything else, so we can take single items from either list. Good
low-cost upgrades back towards v2-real: **the second fan** (~€300–400, comfort in heat),
**a second 200 Ah battery** (+€509, +19 kg), and the **Truma B10** (~€850, hot water).
