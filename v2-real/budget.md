## Decisions taken

- **Starlink stays in** (~€600 with the flat mount), accepted as **EU-only** — it is not
  licensed in Turkey or Morocco.
- **No Cerbo GX or touchscreen** (−€450). Victron's Bluetooth app on a phone shows the same
  things. Can be added later if we miss it.
- **Cushions made by us** (−€400). Foam and fabric are cheap; the cost is the sewing.
- **Rough-road upgrades: tier 1 + 2 yes, tier 3 no** (2026-09-24, can change later) — a separate
  pot of ~€7.4–9.7k, see above.

## Read on that

- Realistic landing zone is **€14–17k**, so at or slightly above the top of the target.
  €10k is not reachable without giving up something real.
- **Electrical is roughly a third of the build.** That is the direct cost of going all-electric:
  no gas means the battery carries cooking and hot water.
- Excluded: tools, the van itself, insurance, and a diesel heater retrofit (~€400 cheap,
  ~€1,500 for a Webasto).

## If it needs to come down further

1. **Top-loading fridge instead of a drawer unit** — saves ~€300 and 0.15 kWh/day, costs daily
   convenience. That convenience is exactly why we did not pick it.
2. **200 Ah instead of 300 Ah** — saves ~€600. I would resist this: that buffer is what makes
   induction cooking and electric hot water survive a cloudy week.
3. **One awning window instead of two** — saves ~€250, costs the cross-flow that replaces
   air conditioning. Bad trade for Morocco and Turkey.
