The real products the build uses or is weighing up. The numbers the model uses live in
[`products.py`](../products.py) — this page is the readable version. Change a size there, run
`python model3d.py v2-real`, and the check says whether it still fits.

Prices in EUR incl. VAT from the shop named, 2026-09-24 (CZK at 25 per EUR). A blank means
not found yet — never a guess.

**optional** = nice to have, first to cut if weight or money runs short.

## Side windows

VW allows cutting only **inside the stamped window fields** of the side panels (converter
guidelines p. 209-210). On our L3 there are two per side, each **~1495 x 720**, from about
750/815 to 1470 above our floor
([ref/vw-crafter-bodybuilder](../ref/vw-crafter-bodybuilder/README.md)). The Crafter-specific
windows below are 1488-1492 x 713 — which confirms that measurement.

| Product | Cut-out | Glazing | Blind / screen | € | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Dometic S4 900 x 450**, top-hinged | 902 x 448 | double acrylic | both built in | 620–680 | **chosen ×2** (dinette, both sides) |
| **Dometic S4 500 x 350**, top-hinged | 500 x 348 | double acrylic | both built in | 260–400 | **chosen ×1** (over the sink) |
| Carbest sliding, Crafter 2017+ front (31710 R / 31711 L) | whole field, 1492 x 713 | single tinted glass | none | 329 | option |
| Globus Camper hinged, Crafter 2017+ side, vent 800 x 350 | whole field, 1489 x 709 | single glass | flyscreen | 490 + fitting 220 | option |

**Why the S4 and not a Crafter-specific window:**

- **Double glazing, blind and flyscreen in one frame.** The Crafter-specific ones found are all
  single glass with nothing built in — we would add a blind, a screen and condensation.
- **The size can match the room.** A whole-field window over the driver front field would run
  behind the shower and the wardrobe. The S4 goes exactly where it is useful.
- **Less glass = less sun.** A whole field is ~1 m² per window; Morocco and summer are the
  point of the trip.

**What the S4 needs:**

- **A frame to the right thickness.** It takes a 27–53 mm wall; ours is ~90–120 at the window
  (cavity + panel). A wooden frame around the cut-out brings it into range — the usual van fix.
- **A flat seat.** The S4 manual asks for a flat wall; the Crafter side leans ~11° and curves
  near the top. The frame takes that up. Dometic's **S7P** is made for curved walls
  (radius 5000) if the S4 will not sit well — worth a look at the real van.

pressing on the front roof panel, which is made for a cut-out.

## Sliding door fly screen

No window in the door (agreed 2026-09-24), but the door is the one opening we will want open
all evening in Morocco, so it needs a screen. Sizes researched 2026-09-24:

| | **VanQuito magnetic net** | Horrex pleated door |
|---|---|---|
| Made for | Crafter / TGE 2017+, **right (passenger) sliding door**, H2 + H3 | Crafter / TGE 2017+, "high roof, door opening over 180 cm" |
| Size | follows the opening (soft net) | **1267 x 1884** |
| How it mounts | sewn-in magnets on the body around the opening | floor profile screwed down at the threshold, top profile clipped on the opening's metal edge under the door seal, pleat cassette at one end |
| Depth into the van | **none** | **not published** |
| Door closes over it | yes, says the maker — but take it off in rain and for driving | not stated |
| Mesh | 0.94 x 0.94 mm, fine (small midges too) | not stated |
| Weight | 0.75 kg | 3.5 kg |
| Price | **€195** | €414–668 (two shops) |

**The size problem with the Horrex:** its 1884 height is made for the front-wheel-drive high-roof
door opening, **1822** tall (VW H508). Our 4MOTION has the high floor, so our opening is
**1722** — 100 lower. It will not fit as sold; Horrex would have to confirm a 4MOTION version.
Its cassette depth is also unknown, and the shoe locker sits just inside the front edge of the
opening.

**Chosen 2026-09-24: VanQuito** — it fits our door as made, costs a third, has no depth to
clash with the locker or the galley end, and weighs almost nothing.

**Rule from Ondrej for any future door screen:** the **shoe locker may get smaller** to make
room; **the galley may not**.
The Horrex stays the nicer option if Horrex confirms a 4MOTION size.

## Full gas — chosen 2026-09-25

Cooking **and** hot water on gas: the van needs no campsite for power (see
[v2-real/energy.md](../v2-real/energy.md)), and solar drops to **2 panels**.

| | Product | kg | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hob | **2-burner gas hob, 30 mbar, flame failure devices** in the old 306 x 527 slot — model to pick (Dometic HB 2325, Lavanda 853, Can Hoodiny 3.9 kg …) | ~4 | proposed |
| Hot water | **Truma Boiler B10**, 10 L, 1500 W, 120 g/h, 350 x 350 x 260 | 6.7 | **chosen** |
| Gas | **6 kg refillable bottle** (~256 x 495) in a sealed locker in the garage, floor vent | ~14 full | **chosen** |
| | regulator, hose, pipes, gas + CO detector | ~6 | |

- **Why 6 kg, not 11 kg:** an 11 kg bottle is ~580 tall; the garage is 570. At ~210 g a day
  (cooking + water) a 6 kg bottle lasts about **4 weeks**.
- **What it brings with it:** a certified gas installation and its periodic inspection (CZ
  "revize"); a flue through the side wall for the B10; LPG refills — easy in Turkey, harder in
  Morocco (bottle systems differ: plan adapters, or refill before crossing).
- **The oven stays electric** (Tefal, 1380 W) — used a few times a week, the battery carries it.

|---|---|---|---|---|
| Isotherm Cruise 85 (proposed) | 475 x 505 x 627 | 85 / ~6 L | 22.0 | easy |

**What changed in the galley to take them:**

- **Fridge:** the 620 height was never the real limit — above it there is room up to ~835
  (the hob wants 65 free under the worktop). So an **85 L** fits: no 80–95 L fridge is under
  622 tall. The Isotherm is the shallow one (505): ~75 mm of air behind it, 92 clear of the arch.
- **Sink:** nobody sells a 440 x 360 sink with a ledge for the tray. The Quadron is 80 deeper
  (440 x 440), which the 565 worktop takes — **so the taps move beside the bowl**, to the back
  of the 340 prep counter, instead of behind it.
- **Oven:** a true hot-air oven, 288 tall. **It fixes a clash v2 had:** v2's 340-tall oven box
  ran 20 mm into the sink bowl. The model now checks built sink parts against the appliances.
  The slot grows 12 wider (462).
- **Power:** set the hob's limit to **2000 W**. Hob 2000 + oven 1380 = 3380 W is more than the
  3000 W inverter: **not both at full power at once.**

**No sink weight is published** — the payload counts 10 kg for sink, taps and filter.
