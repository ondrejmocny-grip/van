# Our build vs a VW Grand California 680 — weight

Researched 2026-09-25. GC figures: ADAC catalogue (VW data), VW Austria, the Czech price list of
31.07.2025, owner weighings (caliboard, vwcaliforniaclub, grandcali.com). Ours:
[v2-real/payload.md](../v2-real/payload.md). **est.** = our estimate, not published by anyone.

## The short answer: VW did not fit it under 3.5 t

| | GC 680 FWD | GC 680 4MOTION | **Ours (4MOTION, manual)** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Offered at 3.5 t? | yes, but **2 seats only** (no rear belts) | **no — 3.88 t minimum** | yes, 3 seats (factory cab) |
| Mass as VW declares it (driver, 90 % fuel, 90 % water, no options, no load) | 3,202 | 3,354 | **~3,150** (3,053 + 97 L water) |
| Paper payload at 3.5 t | 298 | — | ~350 |
| Real, with normal options (owners weighed) | ~3,350–3,400 | ~3,500+ | 3,164 on paper — **weigh it** |
| With 2 people, water and luggage | owners: 3,450–3,790 (**often over**) | owners: up to 4,020 | 3,425 |

- VW's figure is **without options**. Owners weigh their vans **150–200 kg above it** before any luggage.
  VW's own price list says the base van is already heavy and that it "can usually only be fixed
  with the 3.88 t upgrade".
- VW's answer to the weight is **a higher weight limit** (3.88 t or 4.0 t from the factory), not lighter
  parts. We found no "lightweight" claim from VW or the press. That needs a C1 licence above 3.5 t,
  or the Czech B licence exception for motor caravans, which is not certain for us.

## Where the kg are different (conversion only, both on 4MOTION)

GC conversion ≈ 3,354 − (our 2,440 van + ~20 for the automatic, est.) ≈ **~890 kg incl. ~95 L water**.
Ours: 613 + 97 water + 14 gas ≈ **~725 kg**. About **165 kg** lighter — here is roughly where:

| Thing | GC 680 | Ours | Difference (kg) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Heating** | Truma Combi 6 E, 6 kW + ducts | **none** (decided: warm-weather trips) | **−20 to −25** est. |
| **Seats** | 2 swivel cab seats + a rear bench with belts and ISOFIX (at 3.88 t) | factory 3-seat cab, untouched; no rear seats | **−40 to −70** est. |
| **Gearbox** | 8-speed automatic only | manual | **−20 to −40** est. (not verified) |
| **Gas** | locker for 2 × 11 kg bottles (~52 kg full) | 1 × 6 kg (14 kg full) | **−38** when both are full |
| **Bed** | fixed 2.00 × 1.67 m bed: frame, slats, mattress | the dinette makes the bed; cushions only | **−30 to −50** est. |
| **Bathroom** | moulded wet bath, fixed cassette toilet | ply shower + Porta Potti | ~0 |
| **Windows** | more windows + skylights | 3 windows + 2 fans | a little lighter, est. |
| **Battery / power** | 92 Ah AGM (~46 Ah usable), 174 W solar, small inverter | 300 Ah LiFePO4, 2000 VA inverter, 2 × 185 W on roof bars | **+30 to +40** — but **~6× the energy** |
| **Rough-road upgrades** | none | guards, air springs, lift | **+52** |
| **Starlink, tarp** | — | yes | +6 |
| Water | 100–110 L fresh, 80–90 L grey | 108 L fresh, 85 L grey | ~0 |
| Fridge | 70 L | 95 L | +5 est. |

The estimates add up to about −130 to −200 kg, which is consistent with the ~165 kg above.

## Read on that

- **We are lighter mainly because we leave things out:** heating, swivel seats, rear seats, a fixed
  bed, the automatic gearbox and the big gas bottles. That is ~150–220 kg — more than our whole margin.
- **We spend the saved weight on energy and the rough-road kit** (~+90 kg), which the GC does not have.
- **The caution:** the GC's real weight is 150–200 kg over VW's paper figure. Home builds usually come
  out heavier than their spreadsheet too. Our 74 kg margin depends on the estimates — **weigh the
  empty van, weigh a sheet of poplar, and weigh again half way through the build.**
- **If we ever wanted heating:** a diesel heater is ~10–15 kg with its tank line. That still fits, but
  it eats a fifth of the margin.
