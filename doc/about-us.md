# About us and how we'll use the van

Last updated: 2026-09-17

## Who

- **Ondrej** — Czech citizen. 171 cm. Works full-time, remotely, from wherever the van is.
- **Wife** — Turkish citizen. Shorter than Ondrej. Does not need a desk, needs a place to sit with a desk.
- Two people. No dog, 1 cat, 1 small kid, bikes at the back of the car, maybe surfboard on the roofs.
  The bikes go on a **rack on the rear doors**, so nothing else may claim them (2026-09-24:
  that is why the spare wheel stays under the van and the grey tank moved inside).

## How we'll use it

- **Seasonal full-time.** Up to **6 months in one stretch**, then back to a base.
- **Deliberately chasing warm weather.** Not going after cold-weather or winter use.
- Ondrej works a normal working day from inside the van, so the van must be a functioning
  office 5 days a week — that is the single heaviest daily demand on it.
- We expect to **drive regularly**, so alternator charging is a real part of the energy plan,
  not a fallback.

## How we live

- **Very economical with water.** Assume well under the usual 35–40 L/day for two.
- **Very minimal with belongings.** Everything fits in about **70 L per person**, 140 L total.
  This is why storage is not the binding constraint in our layout, unlike most van builds.
- Happy with a **minimal bathroom**: sitting on the toilet, showering sitting down.
- Happy to **leave the rear bed made up** most days and live in the front lounge, rather than
  converting the dinette daily.

## Where

- **Buying the van** in Czechia or Germany.
- **Converting it** in Czechia. Registering it as a motor caravan here.
- **Destinations: the EU, Turkey and Morocco.**

A workable seasonal rhythm falls out of that: **Morocco in winter** (it is a winter vanlife
destination and fits "not cold weather" perfectly), **the EU in summer**, **Turkey in the
shoulder seasons**. Morocco in July would be unbearable with no air conditioning.

### Planning factor: Starlink does not work in two of the three regions

**Starlink is not licensed in Turkey or Morocco** — both sit in pending-regulatory-approval
status. Since Ondrej's income depends on being online, this reshapes the connectivity plan:
outside the EU, 4G/5G is not the primary option, it is the only one. Both countries have good
mobile coverage (Turkey especially), so it works — but the satellite fallback disappears
exactly where the remote places are. Consequence for the build: buy Starlink last, if at all,
and put the money into a proper roof antenna and router instead.

### Planning factor: two different passports

Ondrej is an EU citizen, his wife is not. For 6-month stretches around Europe this matters —
Schengen stay limits and any residence-card requirements need checking before we plan a route,
and they may shape which countries we spend time in. **Verify with the authorities; do not
plan a 6-month route on assumptions.** With Turkey on the list this cuts both ways — also
check the rules on a Turkish citizen driving a foreign-plated vehicle in Turkey, which are
not the same as for a foreign driver.

Morocco adds its own paperwork: the vehicle goes in on a temporary import declaration with a
limit on days per year, and an EU green card does not cover it, so insurance is bought at the
border.
