# Approval in Czechia (STK) — what the conversion needs

Researched 2026-09-25. Sources are linked. **(not verified)** marks what comes only from forums
or our own reading; ask the testing station before relying on it.

## The short version

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Do we need an approval at all? | **Yes.** Cutting windows and fitting the lift are already a "přestavba" (conversion, zákon 56/2001 § 73(2)). An unapproved conversion is a defect at every STK |
| Stay a van (N1) or become a motor caravan (**M1, SA "obytný automobil"**)? | **Become M1 SA** (recommended). Same approval effort; no truck rules (no-truck signs, truck overtaking bans, Austrian weigh stations); usually cheaper insurance; better resale. Czech road tax: none either way |
| Do we pass the weight rule? | **Yes, by 28 kg** when weighed with every tank full, as the testing station does — see [payload.md](../v2-real/payload.md) |
| Cost | ~**10,000–15,000 CZK** in fees and revisions, plus a VW letter (price unknown) |
| Time | 2–4 months with the paperwork (forum reports) |
| **Design changes it causes** | the roof kit goes on **rails on VW's roof-rack points**, not on the roof skin; **straps** for batteries, tanks and the gas bottle; **latches and round corners** on the furniture; a **VW letter** for the roof — see below |

## What a motor caravan must have

EU 2018/858, Annex I, part A 5.1 ([text](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2018/858/annex/I)), and the Czech Ministry of
Transport's method paper ([MD metodika](https://md.gov.cz/getattachment/Dokumenty/Silnicni-doprava/Schvalovani-vozidel/Metodiky/1_2012150METO_2_Priloha_1-(1).pdf.aspx?lang=cs-CZ)):

| Required | Ours |
|---|---|
| Seats and a table | dinette U + table on one post ✓ — **the table may be removable, its mount must be fixed** |
| Sleeping place (may be made from the seats) | the dinette bed ✓ |
| Cooking | Thetford 2-burner gas hob ✓ |
| Storage | lockers, wardrobe, garage ✓ |
| Everything **fixed** to the living space (bolted, screwed, riveted, welded) | the whole build is fixed ✓ — the portable WC and the cushions are loose items, which is normal |

## The steps

Legal basis: zákon 56/2001 § 74, vyhláška 341/2014 § 29. Practical order from
[Tom-Car](https://www.tom-car.cz/prestavba-na-obytny-automobil) (a testing station with a ministry permit for conversions to SA; VW is on their brand list).

1. **Before building: phone the testing station** (zkušebna) and describe the plan. Ask them
   the open questions below. Inspectors differ — pick the station first, then build to what it wants.
2. **Ask VW for a letter of non-objection** through the importer (Porsche ČR) or VW's
   Conversion Portal — see "VW's own rules" below.
3. **Build it 100 %.** A half-finished van is not inspected. **Photograph everything before it is
   covered** (wiring, gas pipe, fixings, straps). Keep every invoice, E-mark and CE paper.
4. **Gas revision** by a certified gas technician, and **electrical revision** (230 V) by a
   revizní technik — see below.
5. The van needs a **valid STK**. Come with **every tank full** — diesel, water, gas. It is weighed.
6. **Testing station check** → it writes the **technický protokol**.
7. **Application at any ORP office** (úřad obce s rozšířenou působností), with the protocol, a
   description of the conversion, drawings, and the proposed changes to the papers. Fee
   **1,000 CZK, 800 CZK online** ([portal.gov.cz](https://portal.gov.cz/sluzby-vs/zadost-o-schvaleni-stavby-nebo-prestavby-vozidla-S778)).
   An STK technician "typu K" checks the van before approval (vyhláška 211/2018).
8. **Tell the insurer.**

**Put everything into one approval:** the camper conversion, the Seikel lift, and any tyre
change. One protocol, one decision, and the weighing covers the finished van (not verified —
ask the station). Seikel supplies a TÜV Teilegutachten (17-00059-CP-BWG-10) and VW's
no-objection letter ([Seikel PDF](https://www.seikel.de/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/CRAFTER_MAN-TG_SEIKEL_Fahrwerke_4motion_Teilegutachten_17-00059-CP-BWG-10_deen.pdf));
Czech stations usually build their protocol on such papers (not verified). Tyre sizes not in
the papers must be written in.

## VW's own rules (body builder guidelines, Nov 2023) — read by us

| Rule | Page | What it means for us |
|---|---|---|
| **Windows:** "cutting the stamped window outline without replacement rigidity is not permitted"; a **letter is required for cutting out stamped window outlines with rigidity measures** (panorama windows) | 7.2.7.2, p. 202 | Our three S4 windows sit **inside** the stamped fields, in a framed cut-out. As we read it, **no letter is needed** — ask for it in the same letter anyway, to be safe |
| **"A letter of non-objection is required for attachments to the roof skin"** (except lights) and for attachments on the roof cross struts | 7.2.10.1, p. 205 | **The fans, solar, Starlink and the tarp rail all touch the roof.** → one letter request covering the 2 fan cut-outs, and the roof kit on rails (next row) |
| "Attachments similar to the roof rack are possible" — high roof: max 150 kg, **at least 3 pairs of feet**, even spacing, ~50 kg per pair | 7.2.10.1 + 7.6.3, p. 270 | **Change:** mount the solar panels, Starlink and the tarp rail on **roof bars on VW's roof-rack mounting points** (or on factory C-rails, PR 3S4, if our van has them) — not glued or screwed into the roof skin. Our ~56 kg (with the bars, ~12 kg est.) needs ≥ 3 pairs |
| Headliner and roof skin must not be changed on a van with **window bags** between the A and B pillars | 7.2.10, p. 205 | only the cab — our cuts are behind the partition. **Check whether our van has window bags** |
| **Centre of gravity above 910 mm** over the road (4MOTION) → the ESC must be re-set, or VW must assess the van | 4.1.2, p. 55 | see "Centre of gravity" below |

## Weight — as the testing station weighs it

EU 1230/2012 (now 2021/535), Annex I 2.6.4.1.2 ([text](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2012/1230/annex/I)):
maximum mass ≥ weighed mass + 75 kg per extra seat + **10 × (seats + length in m)**.

| Reading | kg | Margin to 3500 |
|---|---|---|
| Converted van empty (90 % diesel, driver) + 2 × 75 + 90 | 3342 | 157 |
| + 90 % water + full gas bottle | 3454 | 45 |
| **Every tank full, as Tom-Car weighs** | **3471** | **28** |

Live numbers: [v2-real/payload.md](../v2-real/payload.md). 28 kg is real but thin: **weigh the
bought van per axle first**, and weigh again before the appointment.

## Centre of gravity (VW limit: 910 mm above the road for 4MOTION)

We have no VW number for the van as sold. A rough estimate, **to be measured**:

| | kg | CoG above road |
|---|---|---|
| Van as sold (guess — VW does not publish it) | 2440 | ~750 |
| The build, mostly floor to worktop (floor ~700 above the road) | ~600 | ~1050 |
| Roof kit + bars | ~60 | ~2650 |
| **Converted, empty** | ~3100 | **~840** |
| + Seikel lift | | **~870** |

So probably under 910, **but the lift uses up a third of the margin**. Keep heavy things low
(they are: batteries, tanks, gas, fridge are all on the floor). VW's method to measure it is in
chapter 9.1.2 (weigh with one axle lifted) — the testing station or a VW partner can do it.

## Gas (LPG)

| Item | What is needed | Ours |
|---|---|---|
| Revision | **Required** for a fixed installation (only a 2–5 kg bottle on a ≤ 1.4 m hose is exempt — Tom-Car). A "revizní technik plynových zařízení", to **ČSN EN 1949** ([bydlik-servis](https://www.bydlik-servis.cz/revize-plynu/)) | 6 kg bottle, copper pipe to hob + B10 → **revision needed** |
| Pressure test | 150 mbar, 5 min to settle, 5 min no drop (secondary sources) | the installer's job |
| **Bottle locker** | sealed from the living space, **vent in the floor at its lowest point — mandatory** (Tom-Car). Size: ~2 % of the locker floor, ≥ 20 mm for bottles up to 7 kg (UK sources, **not verified** against EN 1949) | locker in the garage with a floor vent ✓ — size the vent with the installer |
| Sticker | **"LPG" sticker at the rear right** (vyhláška 341/2014, příloha 11, 1b) — also for gas not used for driving | add |
| Detector | not required (not verified) | we fit a gas + CO detector anyway |
| Re-inspection | no legal interval for private owners since 2022 ([vansafe](https://www.vansafe.cz/clanky-o-karavaningu-a-cestovani/plyn-a-elektrika-v-obytnem-aute-povinnost-revize-pojisteni)); every 2–3 years recommended; hoses and regulator max 10 years | plan it |

Our gas design basis for the installer: [v2-real/systems.md](../v2-real/systems.md#gas--for-the-certified-installer).

## 230 V

**ČSN 33 2000-7-721** (HD 60364-7-721). A **revision is required when there is an outside
inlet** (Tom-Car) — we have one. The main points ([IET summary](https://electrical.theiet.org/media/1187/electrical-installations-in-caravan-camping-parks-caravans-and-motor-caravans.pdf)):

| Rule | Ours |
|---|---|
| 30 mA RCD cutting **all** live conductors; an all-pole main switch | 2-pole RCBO (box A) + 2-pole RCD (box B) ✓ |
| Inlet to EN 60309 (CEE), IP44, **max 1.8 m above the ground** | CEE inlet, ~1.1 m above the road ✓ |
| Flexible / stranded cable, ≥ 1.5 mm² | H07RN-F 2.5 mm² ✓ |
| Earth to the inlet, every socket and exposed metal; the body bonded to the main earth | in the plan ✓ |
| A fixed notice by the main switch; a 25 m shore cable with a CEE plug | add |
| **Ask the technician:** how the MultiPlus links neutral and earth when it runs on its own inverter | open |

## Other things that commonly fail (forums)

| Fails | What we do |
|---|---|
| Loose furniture or appliances; doors and drawers that do not latch; **sharp corners** | **push-latches on every door and drawer; all corners rounded** — design rule for the cut list |
| **Batteries, water tanks and the gas bottle not strapped to the body** (Tom-Car) | **straps or brackets on all of them**, fixed to load rails — VW's rivnuts take max 900 N each (p. 332) |
| Seats, belts or their bases changed | we keep the factory 3-seat cab **untouched** ✓ |
| Windows without an **E-mark** | Dometic S4 — **check the E-mark on the glazing** when buying |
| No gas or electrical revision; no floor vent for the bottle | planned ✓ |
| Unfinished van; no valid STK; over the weight limit when weighed full | see above |
| An awning had to come off for the inspection (one forum case) | the tarp is removable ✓ |

Roof fans and solar are usually not checked and not written into the papers (forum) — keep
their papers anyway.

## To ask before we build

1. **The testing station:** do they want a VW letter for our windows (inside the stamped
   fields)? Do they accept the Seikel TÜV papers? Everything in one approval?
2. **VW / Porsche ČR:** one letter for the 2 fan cut-outs and the roof bars on the rack points.
3. **Our van:** does it have **factory C-rails (PR 3S4)** on the roof, and **window bags**? Both
   are in the VIN's PR list (any VW dealer can print it).
4. **The gas installer:** locker vent size; can he sign the revision for our design?
