# Professional van builders by country — prices (researched 2026-09-26)

Question: is there a country where a custom build of our Crafter L3H3 is much cheaper?
**Short answer: no.** Parts (Victron, Truma, windows, fridge) cost the same everywhere; only
labour differs, and Czech builders are already in the same range as their neighbours.
(unverified) = forum or single source.

## Why not Turkey (or Morocco, Serbia, Bosnia)

- The van can enter Turkey on temporary import (up to 730 days), but a **full conversion very likely
  breaks the "unchanged condition" rule** (no official text found — unverified).
- Coming back, the EU "returned goods" relief needs the van **in the same state**. A van (8704)
  becoming a motor caravan (8703) is a changed state.
- **21 % Czech import VAT is due on the value of the work and parts** (VAT Directive art. 88), plus
  duty on the added value under outward processing — which needs a **customs permit before
  leaving**. Duty may be 0 % under the EU–Turkey customs union (unverified); the VAT stays, and the
  builder may add Turkish VAT too (unverified).
- No documented case of anyone doing it legally. Not declaring it is customs and VAT evasion.
- **Result: Turkish price + 21 % + paperwork + risk = no saving.**

## Foreign builder and the Czech approval

The testing station checks the van, not the builder. An EU builder's EN 1949 gas certificate and
E-marked parts help, but expect to **redo the gas and electrical revisions with Czech inspectors**
(a few thousand CZK). A non-EU builder adds non-E-marked parts and foreign paperwork. Ask the
testing station first — see [v2-real/approval.md](../../v2-real/approval.md).

## Compared with our self-build

Our parts alone are ~€16.6–19.9k ([v2-real/budget.md](../../v2-real/budget.md)). A Czech builder at
€15.5–27.5k is for a **standard** layout with simpler parts; our build (300 Ah lithium, gas,
custom layout) would be at the top of that range or above. A realistic middle way: **hire
professionals only for the risky parts** — gas (required anyway), 230 V, window and fan cut-outs.
