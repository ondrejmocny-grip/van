# Weak points and buying checks

[← back to overview](README.md)

## What to check on the van — in this order

| # | Check | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Registration papers, F.1 / F.2** | the real max weight. Expect 2,730 kg, not 3.5 t |
| 2 | **Weigh it** empty, with full fuel | real payload; ≈ 300–450 kg expected |
| 3 | **Roof-to-body joint** (inside at the top of the walls, headliner, lockers) | the Sikaflex joint ages → leaks → rust, cracked roof |
| 4 | **Seitz windows** and roof hatches: cracks, seals, damp wood around them | no spare windows exist |
| 5 | **Floor**: soft spots, smell of damp | hidden water damage |
| 6 | **Rust**: windscreen base, wheel arches, sills, sliding-door bottom, fuel filler, rear cross member, tailgate bottom, where tank pipes go through the floor | T4 + conversion weak spots |
| 7 | **Timing belts** (both) and water pump: proof and date | valves bend if one fails; unknown → change at once |
| 8 | **Cold start, smoke, power on a hill** | VP37 pump seals / electronics, turbo + N75, head cracks at cylinder 3 |
| 9 | **Gearbox**: jumps out, synchros; clutch pedal box cracks | common at high mileage |
| 10 | **Front ball joints, rear arm bushes** | common wear |
| 11 | **Carthago plate** (model 32.2?), **Truma plate** (E 2400 + boiler or C 3402?), **fridge plate** (RM 185?) | confirms what you are buying |
| 12 | Ask them to **run everything**: heater, boiler, fridge on gas and 230 V, pump, taps, WC flush, tank heaters, lights | dealer offers this at handover |
| 13 | **Gas hoses, regulator date** | 29-year-old rubber; the dealer's new revize should replace them |
| 14 | **Leisure battery**: age, type, fuse | probably old lead-acid, maybe no fuse |
| 15 | **Tyre DOT dates** | campers age tyres before they wear them |
| 16 | **Service history**, how many owners, when imported from Germany | 380,000 km needs a story |

## Known weak points

### T4 base vehicle

Sources: [Heritage buying guide](https://www.heritagepartscentre.com/uk/blog/t4-buying-guide.html),
[Heritage tips](https://blog.heritagepartscentre.com/blog/2016/10/18/t4-buying-tips/),
[RWC](https://www.rwcmotorsport.com/tech/tdi-common-problems.php),
[vwt4forum](https://www.vwt4forum.co.uk/threads/axd-axe-head-cracks.106753/).

- **VP37 injection pump**: aging internal seals, quantity-adjuster faults → power loss.
- **Cylinder head**: cracks at cylinder 3 and worn camshafts on some engines.
- **Two timing belts** + water pump: failure bends valves.
- **Charge pressure faults** (code 17964): turbo, vacuum lines, N75 valve.
- **Rust**: windscreen base, arches, sills, sliding-door bottom, filler neck, rear cross member.
- **Wear**: lower ball joints, rear bushes, clutch hydraulics, pedal box, gearbox synchros.
- **Electrics**: instrument cluster regulator (wrong fuel / temperature), chafed door wiring,
  ignition switch, brake-light switch. Loose sliding-door top rail.

### Carthago Malibu conversion

Sources: [t4forum Kaufberatung 32.2](https://www.t4forum.de/forum/index.php?thread%2F189964-kaufberatung-carthago-malibu-32-2%2F=),
[t4forum](https://t4forum.de/forum/index.php?thread%2F270859-t4-carthago-malibu%2F=),
[multifornia](https://www.multifornia.de/index.php?thread%2F3288-t4-carthago-malibu-irgendwo-undicht%2F=),
[womobox](https://womobox.de/forum/thread/4253-malibu-tanks-isolation/).

- **Roof joint leaks** → rust, cracked GRP.
- **Seitz windows** crack, seals leak, **no replacements**.
- **Boiler leaks**; **taps break at the base** (≈ €120 each).
- **Fridge gas ignition** fails; fridge **ventilation is poor** in the 32.2.
- Bench locks, cabinet catches, headliner, roller blinds.
- **Pipes freeze from −8 °C** (under the shower first).
- **Carthago interior spare parts** are rare.
- **Small old charger** (lead-acid only) and **no battery fuse** on early units.
