# Living part — Carthago Malibu 32 (32.3) fit-out

**Correction 2026-09-26 (Ondrej):** this van has **no roof bed** — the space over the cab is lockers. So it is most likely a **32.3** (compact high roof, cabinets, no bed), not a 32.2. The bed is the dinette, made up each night.

[← back to overview](README.md)

No factory data sheet from Carthago is online. What follows is built from **the dealer's
photos**, **the listing**, and **owner forums and old listings of the same model**. Labels as
in the [overview](README.md#read-in-this-order). Numbers from other units can differ from this
one — confirm on the van.

## Which model is it

Malibu was Carthago's van brand (T3 from the mid-1980s, T4 from 1991, its own brand later).
The model number tells the layout ([t4forum](https://www.t4forum.de/forum/index.php?thread/270859-t4-carthago-malibu/),
[T4-Wiki](https://www.t4-wiki.de/wiki/Carthago)):

| Model | Wheelbase | Layout | Roof | Built |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 28.x | short | — | .1 pop-up · .2/.3/.4 high roof variants | 28.1 ≈ 1,600 |
| 31.x | long | kitchen at the rear, **no washroom** | .1 pop-up · .2 high + bed · .3 high, no bed | 700 / 300 / 200 |
| 32.1 | long | kitchen at the rear + **washroom** | pop-up | ≈ 20 |
| **32.2** | **long** | **kitchen at the rear + washroom** | **high roof with roof bed** | **≈ 400** |
| 32.3 | long | kitchen at the rear + washroom | compact high roof, cabinets, no bed | ≈ 200 |

**This van = most likely 32.3**: long wheelbase + high roof with lockers and **no roof bed** (Ondrej) + washroom
(**seen**). Confirm on the Carthago plate inside.

## Layout

Front to rear (**seen**, positions ± — read off photos):

```
  nose ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────── tailgate
  ┌─────────┬──────────────────────┬──────────────────┬─────────────────┐
  │ driver  │ dinette: 2 benches   │ (aisle)          │ washroom        │  driver side
  │ (swivel)│ face to face, table  │                  │ WC, basin,      │
  │         │                      │                  │ shower tray     │
  ├─────────┤                      │                  ├─────────────────┤
  │ passeng.│  aisle               │ kitchen, L-shape │ gas locker +    │
  │ (swivel)│  SLIDING DOOR        │ sink+hob, fridge │ WC cassette at  │  passenger side
  └─────────┴──────────────────────┴──────────────────┴─ the tailgate ──┘
       above the cab: lockers           above both sides: lockers
```

- **Cab seats swivel** (listing) and join the dinette → 4 people at the table.
- **No roof bed** — lockers in the high roof over the cab. (The 32.2 has a wide roof bed that lowers down.)
- **Dinette** turns into the lower bed.
- **Overhead lockers** along both walls under the high roof.
- **Two roof hatches** with skylights (one over the living area, one further back).

**In energy terms:** 60 Ah lead-acid is ≈ 0.35 kWh usable. A work day with a laptop and a
monitor is ≈ 0.4–0.6 kWh. So this van **cannot run an office day off-grid** without new
batteries, a charger and solar.
