none is for sale in Germany today. At ≈ €14.7k it sits **at or below** the German price of a
32.x with the slow 2.4 D. Its weak point is the **380,000 km**; German 32.x ads show 190,000–
263,000 (one at 480,000).

## What the market teaches

- **Weights:** two ads give empty weights: 1,595 kg (short) and 2,035 kg (long, high roof —
  but that dealer ad's data looks generic). Forum units: 2,060 kg, and ≈ 2,240 kg for a 32.2.
  At a 2,730 kg GVW that means **≈ 500–700 kg payload** — better than the 300–450 kg feared in
  [vehicle.md](vehicle.md#weights), but a 32.2 with washroom and tanks is at the heavy end.
  **Still weigh it.**
- **Tanks:** the Rehhorst 32 states **75 L fresh / 50 L grey** — the larger fresh figure.
- **What sellers report most:** rust at sills and arches (welded), broken Truma heaters, roof
  cracks and peeling, cracked windows. Same list as [checks.md](checks.md).
- **Km:** most usable vans show 200,000–350,000 km. 380,000 on the Czech van is high but not rare.
