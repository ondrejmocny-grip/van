## What the market teaches

- **Rare:** only ≈ 600 of all 32.x were built (32.1 ≈ 20, 32.2 ≈ 400, 32.3 ≈ 200). At any time
  only a handful are for sale in all of Germany.
- **Price:** €10,000–15,000 for a usable one with the 1.9 TD or 2.4 D; a TDI one sold at ≈ €17,000.
  The Czech van (TDI, ≈ €14,700) is **at or below** the German price — but has the most km.
- **Tanks:** the Rehhorst 32 states **75 L fresh / 50 L grey** — the larger fresh figure seen in
  the forums.
- **Weights:** no 32.x ad gives one. Forum units: ≈ 2,060–2,240 kg empty. At 2,730 kg that is
  ≈ 500–700 kg payload. **Weigh it.**
- **Engines:** most 32.x for sale have the **1.9 TD (68 PS)** or **2.4 D (77 PS)** — slow with a
  high roof (110–120 km/h, the Rehhorst seller says). The 102 PS TDI is the one to want.
