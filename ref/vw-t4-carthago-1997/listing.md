## Seller description — English

- **Engine:** 2.5 TDI, 5-speed gearbox, **"up to 3.5 t"**, 4 seats for driving.
- **Equipment:** fridge 12 V / 230 V / LPG, cooker with 2 burners, cassette WC, shower,
  Truma hot water and heating, **frost-protection drain valve**, swivel passenger and driver
  seats, air conditioning in the cab.
- **Free services:** 1. gas and electrical inspection (revize) · 2. guarantee of the camper's
  origin · 3. at handover they show every appliance working and walk you through the camper ·
  4. help with financing (ESSOX loans).
- **Viewing:** Trubín 26701, Tovární 150. They take your camper in part-exchange.

**About "up to 3.5 t":** this most likely means "drive it on a B licence", not the plated
weight. VW never made a T4 van for 3.5 t; the highest factory rating is 2,890 kg. The
registration papers (field F.1 / F.2) will say the truth.
