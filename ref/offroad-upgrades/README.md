# Rough-road upgrades for the Crafter 4MOTION — list and assessment

Received 2026-09-24 from Ondrej: a list of recommended upgrades for rough tracks, deep ruts
and unpaved roads. It is kept here in short form, **checked against our van and VW's own
drawing** ([vw-crafter-bodybuilder](../vw-crafter-bodybuilder/README.md)). **Chosen 2026-09-24: tier 1 and 2 yes, tier 3 no — this can be changed later.**

## What the list recommends

| Group | Item | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| Shielding | Front engine + steering guard | 5–6 mm aluminium plate: oil pan, radiator, front diff, steering rack motor (Seikel, delta4x4, GTV-VAN, Rival) |
| | Rear Haldex / diff guard | 5 mm aluminium around the rear coupling and differential |
| | Fuel tank + AdBlue guard | long aluminium plates |
| | Rocksliders / sill bars | steel or aluminium rails on the underbody, so the van slides on them in a rut |
| | Rear shock deflectors | 3 mm stainless plates over the low shock mounts |
| Suspension | Lift kit, e.g. **Seikel +30 mm** | HD springs + tuned dampers (Koni Raid / Bilstein) |
| | HD dampers | Koni Raid, Marquart, Bilstein B6 |
| | Rear auxiliary air springs | VB-Airsuspension / Dunlop, height set from the dash |
| Tyres | All-terrain, 3-ply sidewall | BFGoodrich KO2/KO3, Falken Wildpeak A/T3W, Nokian Outpost AT |
| | Bigger size | 225/75 R16 or 245/75 R16 instead of 235/65 R16 |
| Other | Breather extensions | vents of gearbox, Haldex and diffs moved up into the engine bay |
| | Snorkel | air intake at roof level, for dust as much as water |

## Checked against our van

### Things in the list that are wrong or need care for OUR van

| Claim in the list | What is true for us |
|---|---|
| Upsizing gives "+20–30 mm diameter" | Only for **225/75 R16: +32** (axle +16). **245/75 R16 is +62** (axle +31) and needs the lift kit and a check of the arch liners. Speedometer reads +4.6 % / +8.8 % low. |
| Load index "at least 115/112" | Our van's factory tyres are **C-rated van tyres, around 121/119**. A 115 tyre is **below** that. The rule is: **at least the load index in the van's registration papers (COC)**, not 115. |
| Fuel tank "under the passenger-side sill" | On our **left-hand-drive** van the fuel tank is on the **driver side** (VW underbody drawing). Plate positions follow the real van. |
| Rocksliders "mount to the chassis frame" | The Crafter panel van has **no separate frame** - it is a monocoque. Sliders bolt to the underbody rails; they must be a kit made for the Crafter. |
| Seikel lift "preserves the factory warranty" | Seikel is a VW Commercial Vehicles partner, but **check it in writing** for our van. Our 2023 van may be near the end of its warranty anyway. |

### What changes our measurements

| Upgrade | Inside the van | Outside |
|---|---|---|
| Lift +30 | **nothing** — the body is the same | overall height and step-in height +30 |
| 225/75 R16 | nothing | height +16, step-in +16 |
| 245/75 R16 | nothing | height +31, step-in +31 |
| **Bigger tyres — the spare wheel** | — | **Important.** The spare wheel bay under the rear measures ~730 mm on the VW drawing, for a 712 mm wheel. **A 744 (225/75) is doubtful, a 774 (245/75) does not fit.** We kept the bay for the spare on purpose, so the tyre size is limited by it — or the spare question comes back. |
| Rocksliders | — | may block the **cassette hatch** (driver side, x 700–1150, low) - check the lid can still open; they can also be the **step** at the sliding door |
| Skid plates | — | lowest point a few mm lower (plate + brackets) |
| Air springs | a small compressor and valve, somewhere dry | — |
| Snorkel | — | a pipe up the A-pillar to roof height |

**Step-in:** the slider sill is ~670 above the road today (brochure). With lift and bigger
tyres it becomes **~720–730**. A fixed step, or a rockslider used as one, becomes worth having.

**Overall height:** VW gives ~2500 for the loaded L3H3 4MOTION. Roof fans, solar and rails
add ~100–150, lift and tyres ~50–60 — about **2.65–2.75 m**. Check this against ferries
(Morocco crossings charge by height) and car parks.

### The limit nobody mentions: weight

- A **category B licence allows 3500 kg** total. Going over needs a C1 licence.
- A 4MOTION is already heavier than the FWD van, and our build is heavy on electrics
  (300 Ah, 3000 W inverter).
- This list adds roughly **80–150 kg** (skid plates, rocksliders, bigger tyres, air springs,
  snorkel) — a rough estimate, to check per product.
- **So every kilo here comes out of water, gear and people.** The payload check belongs in the
  product register (step 3), with these items in it.

## Decision 2026-09-24 — can be changed later

**Tier 1: yes. Tier 2: yes. Tier 3: no.** Tyre size stays 235/65 R16 for now (the spare wheel
bay). Priced in [doc/budget.md](../../doc/budget.md): tier 1 ~€3.2–5.0k, tier 2 ~€4.2–4.7k.

Found while pricing:

- **Seikel's breather kit is for manual gearboxes only** — our van is **manual** (confirmed by
  Ondrej 2026-09-24), so it fits.
- **Seikel's rear diff guard is "only for vehicles up to 3.88 t"** — fine for our 3.5 t.
- **The Seikel lift comes with a VW no-objection letter (UBB)**, which is what the technical
  inspection wants to see. It allows up to 50 mm total together with bigger tyres.
- **Weight of tier 1 + 2 is lower than the full list:** ~35–55 kg, because the heavy items
  (rocksliders, long tank plates) are in tier 3. delta4x4 gives its engine guard as 10 kg
  in one place and 15.5 kg in another.

## First take on what fits how we travel

EU, Turkey, Morocco; pistes and unpaved country roads; **not** expedition off-road
([about-us](../../doc/about-us.md)).

| Tier | Items | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **1 — worth it** | A/T tyres (3-ply sidewall), **same size or 225/75 if the spare still fits**; front engine/steering guard; rear air springs | punctures and a hit sump end a trip; air springs fix the sag of a loaded rear |
| 2 — consider | Seikel +30 lift with HD dampers; rear diff guard; breathers | more clearance and control on ruts; breathers are cheap |
| 3 — only if we go harder | rocksliders, fuel/AdBlue plates, shock deflectors, snorkel, 245/75 tyres | heavy, costly, and they solve problems we do not plan to have — except the snorkel, if Moroccan dust turns out to be real |

To decide with Ondrej. Prices and weights come in the product register.
