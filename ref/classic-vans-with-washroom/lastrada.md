## Reading the list

- **In budget, 3.5 t, shower confirmed:** only **L1 Waltershausen 312D, €18,500** — manual,
  ≈ 940 kg payload, TÜV to 2028. **The one to see first.**
- **Ask first:** L2 Ebermannsdorf (€14,700) — is there a shower, and why 3,100 kg?
- **Nova L / Regent L 316 CDI** (L4–L9) are newer, ≈ 6 m long, mostly **automatic**, and **€23–40k**
  — over our budget. Cheapest: L4 Nijkerk €22,999.
- **Automatic:** the Sprinter's **Sprintshift** (L5, L12) has a bad name; the normal torque-converter
  automatic on the 316 CDI is fine.
- **Cheap outlier:** the **Ducato 244 La Strada in Doorn, €15,500, 92,000 km, 5.10 m** — Carthago
  size with a wet room. Worth a look.
- Two "wanted" ads for a Nova L were seen: good ones sell fast.
