## 4. What to be careful about

### Czech Carthago

- **The rear axle, not the total.** Its rear limit is only **1,410 kg**, and nearly everything heavy
  sits at the back: washroom, **water tanks, gas locker, WC cassette**, the kitchen block — and a
  **bike rack on the tailgate** hangs 0.9 m behind the rear wheels, which loads the rear axle
  more than its own weight. **Weigh the rear axle alone.**
- **Bikes:** a tailgate rack must stay within the rack's and the tailgate's own limits (often
  2 bikes, check the rack label). Lighter bikes help more than anything else.
- **No roof load** on the GRP high roof — the surfboard later needs a proper solution.
- **Water:** full tanks = 80 kg. Drive with a half tank when you can fill up where you stop.
- **Heavy things low and between the axles**, not in the rear lockers.

### La Strada V6 Berlin (212D)

- **It is already at the limit with our basic load.** With bikes it is over.
- A 2xx Sprinter **cannot simply go to 3.5 t** — the 3xx has stronger axles, brakes and springs.
  Its 2.95 t is probably already an upgrade. Ask what the papers say and whether more is possible.
- To make it work: **no bikes**, water only half full, very light packing. Not a good match for a
  long trip.
- If we like La Strada: **look for a 312D (3.5 t)** instead — then weight is no worry.

### Both

- **Being overweight** means fines at roadside checks (Germany, Austria and Switzerland check
  campers), possible trouble with insurance after an accident, and longer braking. Brakes and
  tyres on a 30-year-old van have no reserve.
- **Tyres:** check the **load index** on the sidewall matches the axle loads, and use **C (van)
  tyres**. Check the DOT date — older than 6–7 years should be replaced.

## 5. How to check before buying

1. **Registration papers:**
   - CZ technický průkaz / German Zulassungsbescheinigung Teil I:
     - **F.1 / F.2** — max weight
     - **G** — empty weight
     - **7.1 / 7.2** (German) or axle rows (CZ) — max per axle
     - **O.1 / O.2** — towing
   - Photograph the page.
2. **Weigh the van** as you would drive it (full diesel, empty water or known level) at a public
   weighbridge. In CZ: scrap-metal yards, agricultural co-ops, building-material yards. Weigh
   **front axle, rear axle and total** separately — drive on with one axle at a time.
3. **Payload = F.2 − weighed total.** **Rear margin = rear-axle max − weighed rear axle.** Both must
   leave room for ≈ 450 kg total and whatever goes on the tailgate.
