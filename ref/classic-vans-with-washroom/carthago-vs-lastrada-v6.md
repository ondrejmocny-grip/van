# Carthago Malibu 32.x vs La Strada V6

[← back to models and ranking](README.md)

Made 2026-09-27. **Neither maker's factory data sheet is online.** The numbers come from owner
data sheets, forums and ads: the Czech and Rehhorst Carthagos, and the Berlin La Strada V6
([ad](https://www.kleinanzeigen.de/s-anzeige/la-strada-modell-v6-sprinter-212-d-campervan/3485949849-220-3489)).
"(ad)" = one van's ad, "(unconf.)" = not confirmed.

**"V6" is a layout name, not an engine.** La Strada built the V3, V4 and Carat on the short
Sprinter and the **V6**, V6E and Regent on the long one
([La Strada history](https://www.lastrada-mobile.de/presse/40-jahre-la-strada-modellhistorie/)).
Builder: La Strada, Echzell (Hesse), founded 1986.

**In words:** the Carthago is **as wide as a Doblò** and about **one small car-boot longer**
(+0.75 m) — it parks in a normal space, only it is ≈ 0.9 m taller. The La Strada is **a Doblò +
1.2 m long, +10 cm wide, +1 m tall** — it needs a long space and no height barriers.

## What it means

- **Size:** the La Strada is a size bigger — ≈ half a metre longer, 9 cm wider, a bit taller. More
  room inside, a bit harder on narrow roads and in towns.
- **Water:** fresh water about the same (≈ 70 L); the La Strada holds **more grey water** (90 vs 50 L).
- **Fridge:** both came with small absorption fridges. The **Berlin V6 already has a compressor
  fridge**, lithium and solar — the upgrades we would do on the Carthago.
- **Beds:** the La Strada's **lift bed** stays made above the dinette. Only the German Carthago
  **32.2** has a similar lowering roof bed; the Czech 32.3 has none.
- **Weight:** the Berlin V6 is a **212D at 2.95 t → payload ≈ 400 kg**, tight for two people plus
  water, gas and luggage. A **312D (3.5 t)** La Strada, like the one in Waltershausen, has
  ≈ 940 kg. **Ask the Berlin seller for the empty weight** (registration papers).
